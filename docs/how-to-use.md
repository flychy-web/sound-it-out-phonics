# How to use this curriculum

## Pacing

- 3 to 5 short sessions per week
- 10 to 15 minutes per session for Stage Pre, up to 20 minutes by Stage 3
- One skill can take anywhere from 2 sessions to 2 weeks; the child's mastery sets the pace, not the calendar

## Session shape

1. Warm up: 1 to 2 minutes reviewing the previous skill
2. Teach: introduce or continue the current skill using the script
3. Practice: the game, cards, or sound boxes for that skill
4. Quick check: the mastery check item for the skill
5. Stop while it is still fun

## When to move on

Move to the next skill when the child gets roughly 8 of 10 attempts right across two different days. Two days matters; one hot streak is not mastery.

## When a child is stuck

- Drop back one skill and rebuild confidence there
- Shrink the set: fewer letters, fewer words, more repetition
- Keep sessions short and end on a win
- Struggle at Stage Pre is normal and does not predict reading trouble; struggle that persists for months into Stages 2 and 3 is worth discussing with a teacher or evaluator

## What not to do

- Do not encourage guessing from pictures or first letters
- Do not skip ahead because a skill seems easy; the sequence is the product
- Do not turn sessions into tests; this should feel like a game with a grown-up
