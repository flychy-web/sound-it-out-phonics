# Sound It Out

An open phonics scope and sequence that takes a pre-reader from first sounds to first sentences.
Created by Chidinma Omai for [Raised to Resist](https://raisedtoresist.com).
Built for parents and teachers who want structured literacy without an app, a subscription, or anything tracking their kid.

## What this is

Sound It Out is a structured-literacy curriculum organized as a clean scope and sequence: 25 skills across four stages (Pre, 1, 2, 3), moving from oral phonemic awareness through letter sounds and CVC blending to a child reading their first real decodable sentences. Skills are taught explicitly and in order.

One thing most phonics printables cannot claim: every decodable sentence in this curriculum uses only graphemes the child has already been taught, and that constraint is programmatically validated, not eyeballed.

## Who it's for

- Parents of pre-readers, roughly ages 3 to 6
- Homeschoolers starting reading from the ground up
- Microschool, co-op, and pre-K teachers who need a ready sequence
- Anyone who wants science-of-reading instruction on paper, offline, with zero data collection

## The open schema

The `/schema` folder contains the skill sequence and the structure behind it:

- `skill-sequence.json` - all 25 skills in teaching order, by stage
- `curriculum-schema.json` - the structure every skill follows, including the spine rules

You can read the entire sequence before paying anything. That is the point.

## Free sample: Stage Pre

`/sample/stage-pre/` is one complete stage, fully built, free:

- `lesson-guide.md` - all four Stage Pre skills with parent scripts, practice, and mastery checks
- `stage-pre-sample-pack.pdf` - printable game cards, sound boxes, and session guide

Try it with your learner before you buy anything.
